## Projects

### 5 DOF Robotic Arm
- Designed and assembled a 5-DOF robotic arm prototype for precision pick-and-place tasks.
- Implemented embedded motor control and motion sequencing for repeatable movement.
- Calibrated joint actuation and linkage geometry to improve positional accuracy and stability.

### ReLumi Expressive Robot | ESP32, Embedded Systems *(MakeUofT Hackathon)*
- Built a WiFi-enabled robotic companion in a 24-hour hackathon that responds to user inputs with expressive motion and behavior.
- Integrated ESP32-CAM live video, motors, and an OLED interface to enable real-time interaction and feedback.
- Connected computer vision + multimodal AI (MediaPipe/OpenCV with Gemini, ElevenLabs, and AssemblyAI) so spoken requests trigger context-aware actions, mini-games, and emotional states.
- Engineered around unreliable Wi-Fi and streaming constraints through iterative debugging and system-level optimization under tight time limits.
